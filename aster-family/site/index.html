Aster

A family of evidence-first space-engineering projects by Hudson Clavin: a mission planner that shows its sources, a tether-infrastructure study that shows its equations, a parts tool that measures what it makes, and a lab where agents build and honestly grade their own ideas.

The house line, shared by every member: if the system cannot explain why it ranked, screened, or recommended something, it does not present the output as strong.

How they connect

    What every member shares

    • Confidence labels on claims: [Certain] / [Likely] / [Speculative]. PartForge adds a provenance axis (cited / user / tool default) that says who stands behind a number.
    • Provenance on every number: a source, a retrieval date, and where it applies the commit it was computed from.
    • Structured refusal over invention: when the data is absent or out of envelope, the tool says so and says what would help.
    • One canonical parameter source per repo, with everything else reading it (params/rev_a.json, INVARIANTS.md + fixtures, data/tables/*.json).
    • Append-only decision records and one living state file per repo, rewritten at the end of every session.
    • Verify before "done": run the thing and paste the output. A green badge is not a verified behaviour.
    • An honest-assessment section in every public README: what it cannot do, stated plainly.
