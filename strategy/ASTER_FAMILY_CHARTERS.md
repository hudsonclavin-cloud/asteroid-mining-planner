## §0. Charter template

Each charter is one page. All fields required; estimates labeled per house rules ([Certain]/[Likely]/[Speculative]).

- **Lens:** Aster <one noun>
- **Decision question:** one sentence, one clause (if it takes two clauses, the lens is two lenses — master plan §7)
- **State / Wave:** CONCEPT | CHARTERED | FOUNDED | BUILDING | SHIPPED | SUNSET · wave assignment
- **Entry gate:** the specific event permitting CHARTERED → FOUNDED (named user, data partner, application deadline, or mothership seat assignment)
- **Kill criteria:** conditions for demotion/sunset (demotion is free and logged)
- **Substrate reuse:** which of S1–S4 it consumes, estimated %
- **New-domain import, priced honestly:** what must be learned and verified that the substrate does not contain
- **Market class & buyer-today:** per master plan §4 discipline, labels required
- **Charter provenance:** authoring project, date; amendments appended below the entry, never edited in place
