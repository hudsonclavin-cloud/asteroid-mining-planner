### The Refusal Contract - one honesty contract, two instruments, one measurement

**One sentence.** Aster is a family of engineering instruments built on one contract - every number carries its provenance and confidence class, and when the data is absent the tool returns a structured refusal with what would help - plus the only pre-registered, DOI-sealed measurement (to Hudson's knowledge) of whether AI agents keep that contract; the artifact that proves it is a short two-domain paper with its two datasets, astrodynamics (already collected, 468 runs) and machined rocket parts (PartForge, not yet run), published as the evals/honesty-study/REPORT.md that Slice 16 promised and never delivered.
